# Awesome Design.md Skill

This skill provides modular design system specifications (`design.md`) and token guidelines to eliminate boring, generic AI interfaces.

## Key Design Blueprints
1. **Design Tokens & Theme Scales**:
   - Primary: Electric Indigo (`#6366f1`), Vivid Violet (`#a855f7`), Cyan Accent (`#06b6d4`).
   - Surfaces: Glass cards with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
   - Typography: Font scale from `Space Grotesk` (Headings) + `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Body).

2. **Component Blueprints**:
   - **Hero Pulse**: Animated live glow indicators and statistical counter cards.
   - **Bento Grids & Interactive Cards**: Elevated hover states with subtle `translateY(-4px)` and shadow blooming.
   - **Status Badges**: High-contrast, semantic pills with soft background fills.
   - **Ticket Passes**: Boarding-pass format with perforated tear-line styling and scannable QR canvas.

3. **Responsive Grid Architecture**:
   - `grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fill, minmax(340px, 1fr))` for fluid adaptation on mobile, tablet, and widescreen.
